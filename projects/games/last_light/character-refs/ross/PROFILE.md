## C. Personality and psychology
**Canon summary** (M:170): "Calm, courteous, unhurried. Steel underneath. Capable of deliberate cruelty, and ashamed of it."

**Five defining traits** `[IMG]` §3:
1. **Measured.** Speaks slowly and precisely, with the patience of a man who has pulled through foul weather and long work.
2. **Principled.** Values the truth, and the honour of his crew, above his own reputation.
3. **Practical.** Turns quickly to action, to what can be done rather than what might have been. At the jam: "Motor off, and we'll back her off it by hand."
4. **Courteous.** Old-fashioned respect in speech, even when accusing. He keeps a seaman's manners.
5. **Resolute.** Once a course is set, he doesn't turn from it, whatever the cost to himself.

**Motivation:**
- **Canon** (M:171): "The truth written in the light's own book, in the hand that wrote the lie, before he dies. And to see his girl's ship past the Gut."
- `[IMG]` §4:
  - have the truth entered in the lighthouse's own log;
  - make sure Elspeth's ship passes the East Gut safely;
  - face the man who wrote the false entry and give him the chance to make it right;
  - leave the matter settled before his health fails further.

**Fears:**
- **Canon** (M:172): "That he did miss the light. That Elspeth won't want him."
- `[IMG]` §5:
  - that he truly did miss the light, and has spent eighteen years comforting himself otherwise;
  - that he will die before the truth is recorded;
  - that Elspeth won't be able to forgive his long absence, whatever the explanation.

**His own guilt:**
- "I had them racing for home … I pushed her. That much is mine."
- "I let Alasdair Ross stay dead."
- His friendship with Iain is the part he "never managed" (ch5:68).
- He forgives Callum the boy ("You were twenty-three … God help me, so did I at that age"), but not the eighteen years.

## I. Mystery and continuity
- **He knows:**
  - the light was out at 01:52, when he sent the Mayday, and the wreck was at 01:58;
  - the official record is false, and Callum wrote it in his own hand, which Ross recognised in 1969;
  - from Kenny, that Iain left something "in the desk";
  - Iain is dead (from the Gazette).
- **He doesn't know:** the letter's text, until Callum hands it to him (ch5, `gave_letter`).
- **He hides:**
  - early: that he survived and lived as Sandy Morrison; why he stayed away from his family;
  - throughout: the full extent of his illness.
- **Knowledge by chapter** (M:279):
  - ch1: everything except the letter's text;
  - ch2: hears 16 all night, including Morag;
  - ch5: the letter's text, if handed it.
- **Grounded explanations he gives in ch5:**
  - the carrier at dusk was his test;
  - the calls were his low-power set;
  - the 046 echo was his boat in the Gut;
  - the dead 16 was his keyed handset.

  The jam is not his doing: it is the worn tooth.
- **Death count** (F19): eight aboard; six of his crew died; he and Kenny lived. The world counts seven lost, himself among them.
