## C. Personality and psychology
**Canon summary** (M:136): "Brave and chatty. Superstitious. Talks over himself. Wants the old men's respect."

**Five defining traits** `[IMG]` §3:
1. **Brave.** Willing to keep going in poor weather when others might turn back.
2. **Chatty.** Fills the air with talk, stories and plans, often before he's finished the last thought.
3. **Superstitious.** Keeps the small seafaring customs: won't whistle on deck, wary of ill omens.
4. **Impetuous.** Acts quickly and speaks faster still, which can lead to mistakes he then rushes to correct.
5. **Eager to belong.** Wants the older men's respect, and to be seen as a proper skipper, not "the boy with his father's boat".

**Motivation:**
- **Canon** (M:137): "Get the catch home. Not be 'the boy who lost his father's boat'."
- `[IMG]` §4:
  - get the catch home in one piece;
  - show he can handle a hard night and a difficult sea;
  - be counted among the island's skippers, not pitied as a youngster;
  - **deeper:** live up to his father's reputation and keep *Kittiwake* in the Bain family.

**Fears** `[IMG]` §5:
- Being the boy who lost his father's boat, through poor judgement, bad luck or looking away at the wrong moment.
- Letting the crew down, even if it's only one other man.
- That the old stories about the *Mary Ellen* carry a truth he doesn't want to test.

**What he hides** `[IMG]` §6: how much the *Mary Ellen* story unsettles him. He repeats it half in jest and half in belief. Canon (M:138): he conceals nothing deliberately.

**Emotional texture:**
- shows feeling openly and immediately (M:193);
- his warmth is the counterweight to the night;
- under the chatter is a young man missing his father and grandfather, within a year of each other's memories.

| Emotion shown | Openly, immediately |
| Pattern | Says a thing twice ("Holding. Holding.") |
| Under pressure | Gets faster, loses "over" |
| Would never say | Anything formal or bitter |

**Dossier voice** `[IMG]` §8:
- **Rhythm:** fast, energetic, often running ahead of himself; sentences trail off, restart or correct.
- **Vocabulary:** island speech, warm and informal: "och", "aye", "eh", "mind", "right enough", "so it is", "over".
- **Radio:** correct basic procedure (identifies vessel, gives position), but wordy, and needs gentle steering back to essentials.
- **Tics:** "och…", "eh, sorry…", "so, what I'm saying is…", "right, over". Laughs awkwardly when he's talked too much.
- **Never says:** a long, solemn speech; "the sea took him for a reason"; any claim to know the full truth about 1953.

- **The legend: Ross's ghost boat, Rory's version.** Adapted from the dossier §10 to canon (F08: Kenny *came home* and told it). This is the reference text for any retelling:
  > The story is the skipper, Alasdair Ross, wasn't found with the rest, and some say he never went down at all. On nights like this, when the wind's out of the south-west, you can see his boat off Pabbay: no lights, just a dark shape keeping to the lee, as if he's still trying to make North Point, looking for the light that wasn't there.
  - **Canon form** (ch1:83): "my grandfather used to say there's a boat out in the East Gut on nights like this. No lights. Just waiting."
  - **How it differs from the truth:**
    - the legend says Ross died and his boat returns as a restless spirit;
    - in truth Ross survived as Sandy Morrison and tonight's dark boat is the rebuilt *Catriona*, SY 214;
    - "Kenny knew fine whose boat it was" (ch5:74).

## I. Mystery and continuity
- **Knows:**
  - his grandfather was the *Mary Ellen*'s engine-man and the only member of her crew who came home;
  - the island legend of Ross's boat;
  - Kenny's words "She's not finished, Ruaridh."
- **Doesn't know:**
  - that Kenny met Ross in March 1970;
  - that Ross is alive;
  - that the 1953 record is false.

  He never learns these on air. In ch6 Callum can only hint ("Tell me after").
- **Knowledge by chapter** (M:277):
  - ch1: the legend;
  - ch3: saw the dark boat;
  - ch6: standing by.
- **Mystery function** `[IMG]` §13:
  - his family link keeps the wreck alive in local memory and gives the legend weight;
  - his sighting near Pabbay is the practical lead to the Visitor's arrival;
  - his half-belief in the ghost boat shows how the false account turned a real injustice into a haunting;
  - standing by shows loyalty and courage.

## J. Development constraints
- **Writers:**
  - Keep him warm, fast and self-correcting.
  - Never solemn or bitter.
  - He calls Callum "Callum".
  - The legend is local lore, never presented as true. **Nothing supernatural.**
  - Never let him claim knowledge of 1953 beyond Kenny's stories.
- **Continuity:**
  - *Kittiwake* is **SY 37**, always.
  - Kenny **survived** (F08).
  - His father was lost in 1968, a separate tragedy.
  - His mother is alive.
- **3D:** freckles, curls, wool cap, oilskin over a guernsey. Young but weathered. The gap tooth shows only in the grin.
- **AI agents:** ignore the "SY 76" and "lost that night" in the dossier image.
