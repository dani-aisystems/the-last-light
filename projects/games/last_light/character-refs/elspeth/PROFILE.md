## C. Personality and psychology
**Canon summary** (M:156): "Exact, cool, very competent. Ice when she hears 'North Point'."

**Five defining traits** `[IMG]` §3:
1. **Exact.** Keeps precise records, checks a course twice, and expects the same of others.
2. **Cool.** Rarely shows emotion on watch; decisions are calm and considered, even under pressure.
3. **Very competent.** Confident on the bridge, practical with machinery, decisive when the wind shifts.
4. **Guarded.** Keeps personal matters to herself; professional to the point of frost.
5. **Principled.** Values competence and responsibility over sentiment.

**Motivation:**
- **Canon** (M:157): "Her ship, her crew, her name."
- `[IMG]` §4:
  - **Immediate:** get the *Iona Star* and her crew safely to port.
  - **Personal:** test the letter's claim. Could her father not have been to blame?
  - **Deeper:** restore her father's name, and her own, by knowing the truth and acting on it.

**Fears:**
- **Canon** (M:158): the East Gut, and that the letter is someone's cruelty.
- `[IMG]` §5:
  - the East Gut, the place that took her father and made her family a byword for disgrace;
  - being proved wrong: that the letter is false hope and her father was at fault after all;
  - trusting the wrong man, so that seeking the truth brings harm on her crew.

**What she remembers of her father** `[IMG]` §12:
- a kind, practical man, more at ease on deck than ashore;
- patient with her, letting her take the helm in calm weather: "Hands steady, Elspeth, and look ahead, not down";
- not a drunk, as the rumour had it, but a man who cared for his crew and kept a tidy boat.

She does **not** remember the night of the wreck, only the news and what came after: her mother's silence, the looks in the village, and the decision to leave.

**Inner contradiction:** she has built her life on proving the Ross name can be trusted, and the man she must trust tonight is the keeper whose station condemned it.

**Dossier voice** `[IMG]` §8:
- **Rhythm:** clipped, controlled, economical.
- **Vocabulary:** maritime and practical: "course", "heading", "steering", "drift", "make the land", "acknowledge".
- **Radio:** flawless procedure, clear identification, repeats critical information, no wasted words.
- **When tense:** her voice cools to near-impersonal. **First names are rare** (F10):
  - "Mr Mackay" is the norm;
  - "Callum Mackay" only in extremity (ch6:7);
  - never a bare "Callum".
- **Verbal tics:** none. She avoids hesitation and filler. Her only marker is the clipped `...` before a sentence she cuts off.
- **Never says:**
  - sentimental appeals;
  - excuses for poor seamanship;
  - anything that diminishes her father as a man;
  - "please" on the radio.

## I. Mystery and continuity
- **She knows:**
  - the official account that blamed her father;
  - the local scorn that followed;
  - that the unsigned letter says the light was out;
  - Callum's name, from ch3.
- **She hides:** that she has kept the letter for a month and has been quietly seeking confirmation. She tells Callum about it in ch3, or at the latest in ch6:9.
- **She doesn't know** until ch6 (reunion path only):
  - that her father survived, or that he sent the letter;
  - that Callum wrote the false entry. She never learns this on air, only in the epilogues.
- **Knowledge by chapter** (M:278):
  - ch3: the letter, and Callum's name;
  - ch6: possibly her father.
- **Death count** (F19): she believes seven men died, her father among them.
- **Mystery function** `[IMG]` §14:
  - the letter brings the past into the present;
  - her family history shows how the false account shaped a generation;
  - her decision to trust Callum's directions decides whether the ship survives;
  - she gives the wreck a human consequence beyond the logbook.

## J. Development constraints
- **Writers:**
  - Procedure first. "Mr Mackay", never a bare "Callum".
  - No "please" on air.
  - No stated feelings before ch6.
  - Emotion shows only in cut-off sentences.
  - Never let her diminish her father.
- **Knowledge:** she learns her father is alive only through the ch6 reunion. Never before.
- **Rank:** she is the mate. The master is injured. She is never "the skipper".
- **3D:** grey eyes, dark hair pinned back, a lean and composed face. Not glamorous, a working officer.
- **AI agents:**
  - ignore the dossier's ch1–2 and ch4 radio presence;
  - don't use its "Callum" sample line as written.
