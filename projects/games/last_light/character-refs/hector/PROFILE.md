# Hector Macleod: secondary profile

**DRAFT 2026-11-06.**
- Hector isn't one of Daniel's six principals (F13), but he has radio scenes in ch2 and, optionally, ch3.
- Built **only from established canon** (M:142-149 and the shipped dialogue). No dossier or written bible exists (G03), so nothing here is invented.

---

## A. Core identity
| Field | Detail |
|---|---|
| Name | Hector Macleod |
| Age | About 68 (1971), so born c. 1903 |
| Occupation | Master of the schooner *Gannet*, **SY 112**, running passengers and cargo round the Harris coast for forty years |
| Radio card | "GANNET SY 112", "Schooner · master H. Macleod" (speakers.gd) |
| Role | An old-generation witness. The one who innocently says "Sandy Ross" (M:145) |

## B. Background
- Knew Iain well, and knew the summers when Ross ran the Board's stores and sat up with Iain (M:146).
- "Sandy and Iain, thick as thieves, the summers Sandy ran the Board's stores." (ch3:123)

## C. Personality
- Courteous, unhurried, old-fashioned (M:147).
- Forgets that the dead are dead: "Tell your father I was asking for him. Oh. Och, I'm sorry, Callum. Thirteen years and I still forget." (ch2:57)

## D. Voice
| Aspect | Rule |
|---|---|
| Sentence length | Long and rolling |
| Formality | Old courtesy ("I'd take a bearing kindly") |
| Vocabulary | "Grand job", "och", sailing-ship terms |
| Emotion shown | Warmly, then embarrassed |
| Pattern | Apologises for the dead |
| Under pressure | Prays, a little |
| Would never say | Anything cruel |

**Canon lines:**
- "I've two passengers sick as dogs and no stars to steer by. I'm somewhere west of you, I think. I'd take a bearing kindly. Over." (ch2:48)
- "Snug as a dog in a basket, North Point." (ch3:118)
- "Nobody on Harris said the name for a long while. Gannet out." (ch3:128)

## E. Relationships
- **Callum:** knew his father; warm and paternal. Callum remembers him as someone who "knew his father" (M:114).
- **Iain:** an old friend. On a night like this Iain "would have had the kettle on and the tide tables open" (ch3:119).
- **Ross:** knew him as "Sandy". He believes Ross drowned.

## F. Narrative role
- **ch2:** lost west of North Point with sick passengers; Callum brings the *Gannet* in by bearing ("Grand job, North Point").
- **ch3 (optional call):** lying to two anchors in the West Gut lee. He remembers Iain and "Sandy Ross", which gives the player the nickname behind the alias.

## I. Mystery and continuity
- He knows nothing of the 1953 lie, and believes Ross is dead.
- His only function in the mystery is the innocent "Sandy Ross" link (ch3), and the reminder that Iain is thirteen years dead (ch2).

## J. Constraints

- Never let him know or hint at the truth.
- No new backstory without Daniel's material.
