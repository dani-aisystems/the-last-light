THE LAST LIGHT
A game by Daniel Yanchev

North Point Light, Outer Hebrides. The middle watch, 14-15 November 1971.

A first-person narrative game. You are Callum Mackay, principal keeper of a lighthouse on a
rock off the Hebrides, keeping the night watch through a severe gale. You keep the lamp
turning, the generator running and the batteries charged, and you talk the fishing boats
home through the reefs by radio.

Then a voice on Channel 16 gives a call sign that was lost with eight men in 1953: the night
this same light went dark for eleven minutes. Before dawn, someone will climb the stair.

Four endings. Play time: about [MEASURED IN R6] minutes. Progress saves automatically;
Continue on the title screen resumes.


OPENING THE GAME ON macOS
-------------------------
Requires macOS 11 or later on Apple silicon, or macOS 10.15 or later on an Intel Mac with a
Metal graphics card. The game is not notarised by Apple, so macOS blocks it the first time:

  1. Unzip TheLastLight.zip and double-click "The Last Light".
  2. macOS says it cannot verify the app. Click "Done" (not "Move to Bin").
  3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down to "Security", and click
     "Open Anyway" next to "The Last Light was blocked". Confirm with your password.
  4. The game opens. From then on it opens normally.

  On macOS 14 or earlier you can instead right-click the app, choose "Open", then "Open".
  From Terminal, this also works:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "The Last Light.app"


CONTROLS (keyboard and mouse; a gamepad also works)
--------
  Mouse              look
  W A S D            walk (Shift: hurry)
  E                  use, pick up, answer the radio
  Esc / right click  step back from a view; pause
  1 - 6              radio orders to the selected vessel (Tab: next vessel)
  J                  tap: the current duty  /  hold: a hint
  L                  the station log
  H                  dialogue history
  T                  transcription of a document you are reading
  B                  binoculars
  G                  hand compass
  F                  torch
  F11                fullscreen

Dialogue advances by itself at reading speed. Options > Accessibility sets the reading time,
the text size, reduced flashing, and Guidance (how much help the duty line gives).
Options also has Graphics (quality preset), Audio and Controls.
