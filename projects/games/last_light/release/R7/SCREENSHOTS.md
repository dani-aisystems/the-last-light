# THE LAST LIGHT: submission screenshots (R7)

The eight images are in-engine captures of the shipping game: Godot 4.7.2, the **High** quality preset, a 1920×1080 window. They were made with `capture_fp` (the game's own capture states) on 2026-11-20/21. Image 3 is a frame from the in-engine chapter 1 film, an automated keeper playing through the real controls, also at High. The pixels are unaltered: no sharpening, grading or retouching.

Each image is in `submission/` twice: a full-size PNG, and a 1280-wide JPEG (quality 92) for web forms. The untouched originals are in `originals/`, and `TheLastLight_contact_sheet.png` shows all eight together.

| # | File | Why it is in the set |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `TheLastLight_01_title_menu` | The first thing a judge sees: the title over the live exterior, the beam turning in the rain, and the game's own logotype and menu. |
| 2 | `TheLastLight_02_watch_room_dusk` | The world. The 1971 watch room, built for the game from reference photographs: lamp-lit desk, radar, optic column, rain on the glass at dusk. |
| 3 | `TheLastLight_03_lamps_come_up` | Real interaction. The keeper's hand on the torch, the charge-switch prompt, his own log entry ("16.22 Batteries flat. Engine started, on charge.") and his thought as the lamps come up. |
| 4 | `TheLastLight_04_radio_morag` | The narrative system. Morag's radio card and line on Channel 16, the six radio orders, the live radar and the chart: story and simulation on one screen. |
| 5 | `TheLastLight_05_storm_tower` | Atmosphere and weather: the gale, rain, the beam and a lightning strike over North Point. |
| 6 | `TheLastLight_06_man_at_the_door` | Tension, in chapter 4. The stair door opens on the red stairwell light and the man at the door stands in it, his face unseen. This is from the real story run (`--bot=expert`, sim 2020 s), not a staged state. |
| 7 | `TheLastLight_07_dawn` | Lighting: cool dawn light against the last warm lamp, as the night ends. |
| 8 | `TheLastLight_08_ending_heard_together` | Emotional payoff and typography. One of the four endings: its panel and epilogue, set in the game's own type. |

**Rejected candidates, kept for reference in `renders/last_light/r7/screens/png/`:**
- the storm through the binoculars: from the seated radio pose the view is cut by the window mullions, and a radio card covers it;
- the generator-crank and console prompts: legible, but too dark at that point in the night;
- the main menu with "Continue" focused: it shows a test-save leftover (F5).
